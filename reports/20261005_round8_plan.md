## 2. The corrections R8 forces (all verified, all adopted)

### 2.1 Our DECISION_LAYER preregistration had a real design flaw

A common strictly monotone transform preserves argmax.  Therefore:
D0 ("ceiling of any monotone decision rule over frozen B3 scores")
equals B3's own argmax except ties - it measures nothing.  D1
("monotone calibration, then argmax") has EXACTLY zero gain by the
same identity.  Our prereg also used D0 as a router for the 7B exam;
that logic cannot work (monotone invariance holds for every input, so
it says nothing about stronger models).  D0/D1 are replaced (unit test
only / deleted), D2 demoted to risk diagnostic, D3 subsumed by the
exact full-information arm.  Full record:
reports/r8/decision_layer_correction.md.

This is the second round in a row where an outside review found a
pre-registered design error before we spent CPU on it (R6: z-score
indexing; R7->R8: vacuous monotone arms).  The critique obligation in
the V8 prompt paid for itself.

### 2.2 The Ahmed-2015 argument misfired in our setting - the real suspect is our objective

Our labels are ALREADY candidate-utility means (u_j = mean_a
IoU(b_j, Y_a); RV: 6 annotators, LIVE: 1).  Argmax over predicted
conditional mean utility IS the Bayes-optimal risk-neutral decision.
The Ahmed gap applies to box-coordinate mean regression, which we
never did.  What deviates from the Bayes rule in our stack: B3 trains
huber(0.25) + 0.3 pairwise-best-vs-worst, not L2 on u.  Huber's
population optimum solves E[clip(u_hat - U, -0.25, 0.25) | x] = 0 -
not the conditional mean - and the pairwise term perturbs it further.
Order inversion is possible (bundle counterexample), and nobody has
tested it on our table.  That test is C/M/E:

- C = current objective (huber+pair)
- M = pure L2 conditional-utility regression
- E = full-information expected utility (softmax exact policy
  gradient over all 36 known rewards, optional KL beta in {0, .03, .1})

All-candidates-known means NO sampling gradient is needed: the exact
policy gradient is p_j (r_j - r_bar).  36 IoUs on one frame are 36
evaluations of ONE label, not 36 annotators; group-relative sampling
adds variance, not information.  GRPO/RLOO stay behind the
enumeration-infeasibility condition, which our candidate table does
not meet.  Gates: +0.015 vs B3, source-level paired CI lower > 0;
E must beat M (not only C) to claim the RL-style objective matters.
Cost: ~6 CPU-h.  This is now the top-priority probe.

### 2.3 The annotation-ceiling hypothesis loses its strongest number

Independent re-read of the LIVE-YT-VC paper (arXiv 2604.24947)
confirms R8: the ~0.50 IoU is between boxes on CONSECUTIVE frames
(temporal-smoothness statistic; consecutive frames may be labeled by
different subjects) - NOT same-frame inter-annotator agreement.  Our
R6 ledger line "adjacent-annotator IoU ~0.50" is corrected in the
ledger.  Consequence: "B3 0.529 ~= human ceiling" had no direct
support; the 0.247 oracle gap is again UNBOUNDED by that number, and
Part B (estimation/decision error) is back on the table as a live
question - to be measured by C/M/E, not assumed in either direction.

Two more facts from the same re-read, both recorded:

- LIVE-YT-VC++ (temporally filtered, ~8.1 subjects/frame) is still
  "Coming soon" upstream (verified today).  No data upgrade available.
- New anchor (paper Table IV, flexible/non-height-preserving protocol,
  the type our 9:16 task belongs to): STCAT 52.3 / CG-STVG 53.1 mIoU;
  from-scratch models collapse toward center bias.  B3 0.529 sits at
  published-model level on this dataset family.  Different splits -
  a ballpark anchor, not a bound, and NOT a ceiling proof.

### 2.4 Reward and method answers (Q2/Q3 collapsed into one recipe)

Supervised conditional-mean regression + argmax and unregularized RL
are the SAME population objective; they differ only through finite
sample, optimization, model class, and objective mismatches - and our
only mismatch is C's huber+pair.  So: keep reward = raw candidate IoU,
weight 1, no sweep; KL 0.03 as a design-value regularizer; no entropy
bonus, no group-std normalization, no format reward (hard-constrain
outputs instead), no teacher reward until the teacher pilot passes.
Baselines do not remove position shortcuts - guards stay: shuffled
candidate IDs, content-permutation controls, source-level splits.

The expected-F-as-RL-reward question: fixed-budget exact expectation
is a NEW scope, not a silent reopen of the closed line; operator
approval required.  The ~28-point temporal figure stays classified as
an early back-of-envelope; no budget against it.

### 2.5 SFT/OPSD are NOT exhausted; the 7B arm changes shape

7B has never been supervised-adapted on this task - that is the
evidence-poor direction with the cleanest interpretation.  FD-OPSD's
selective distillation provably degenerates to 0 for single-token
actions (N=1 centering); the transplantable form is categorical KL
from a dense-view teacher, and only after dense view BEATS sparse view
on real labeled IoU.  32B teacher: unproven for this task; pilot gate
+0.02 with CI lower > 0 on 100-200 dev sources; 32B outputs are
forbidden in any final-inference dependency chain.

New primary 7B arm: VLM_SFT (constrained candidate-ID readout, frozen
vision tower, LoRA r=8 q/v, lr {5e-6, 1e-5}, effective batch 8, 12
NPU-h cap, gate +0.02 vs strongest confirmed baseline).  The R7
zero-shot exam (V1-V3) is demoted to a spare-budget evidence probe -
SFT directly targets the gate the exam could only read.
