## 3. What the S-line changes strategically

Two independent lines now point at the same conclusion:

1. F1 arithmetic: 34.95 = all-frame prediction necessity
   (2 x 0.62 x 0.39 / 1.39); ~28 points of the gap live in frame-level
   ranking = the temporal line.  Spatial is also structurally divided
   by the 159/426 no-slide videos.
2. S-REREAD + the annotator-disagreement hypothesis: the 0.247 spatial
   oracle gap may be mostly mean-annotator vs single-annotator
   disagreement (paper-reported adjacent-annotator IoU ~0.50 ~= our B3
   0.529).  If true, NO backbone - including a 7B VLM - can learn past
   it, and the official spatial headroom is bounded by the same
   statistics.

Therefore: the 9B budget tilts from "see the crop better" (R7's first
choice) to "rank frames better" (temporal / joint).  The spatial VLM
exam survives, but as the cheap instrument that separates
"capacity-limited" from "annotation-limited" - not as the main bet.
And the FIRST spend is neither: it is the 0-NPU ceiling measurement.

### 4.1 Ceiling measurement - human micro-study (0 NPU-h, ~1 day wall)

The direct per-annotator test is impossible on public data (verified).
Replacement, pre-registered in reports/r7/preregistration.yaml:

- Sample 120 frames: 60 from dev80 (labels + B3 scores + crops all
  cached) + 60 from the 76 never-used sources (bonus fresh B3 read).
- >= 2 (target 3) independent human annotators draw ltrb boxes, no
  model output shown, fixed written instructions, source-pixel coords.
- Readouts: H = mean pairwise human-human IoU; BH = mean IoU(B3, human).
  delta = BH - H with paired CI over frames.
- Decision rule (frozen): delta >= -0.03 -> ceiling-supported (B3
  already at human agreement; spatial VLM line demoted to diagnostics);
  delta <= -0.10 -> gap-supported (keep the spatial VLM line as a real
  arm); between -> INCONCLUSIVE, escalate to 4.4.
- Needs operator participation (humans).  Tooling on request: frame
  export + a minimal local labeler page.  If no second human is
  available, 4.4 becomes the only separator.
