## 1. Expected-F three-seed confirmation (DONE)

Design: paired arms (same TCN, same sampler stream), MSE incumbent versus
exact expected-F DP, three seeds, frozen valid-token features, 1,963
train / 1,954 held-out mixed fragments, keep-0.80 simulated F1.

| Arm | seed 20261006 | seed 20261007 | seed 20261008 | mean +- sd |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| MSE keep-0.80 F1 | 0.6273 | 0.6276 | 0.6514 | 0.6354 +- 0.0138 |
| exact-DP keep-0.80 F1 | 0.6625 | 0.6626 | 0.6625 | 0.6625 +- 0.0001 |
| paired delta | +0.0352 | +0.0350 | +0.0111 | +0.0271 |

Per-fragment paired bootstrap (seed 20261006, 10,000 resamples):
delta +0.0352, CI95 [+0.0291, +0.0413].  The CI lower bound is 4x the
preregistered practical delta (0.007).

Readings:
1. The expected-F gain is stable across seeds (3/3 positive).
2. The DP arm's seed variance is near zero (sd 0.0001 vs MSE 0.0138):
   directly maximizing F converges to the same solution regardless of
   initialization, while the ranking surrogate scatters.  A second,
   unplanned, advantage of optimizing the deployment metric.
3. AP moves the same direction (+0.018 to +0.041 paired per seed).

Validity scope: PHD2 in-domain; the 984 held-out sources are
dev-exposed (V11 section 1.5), so this is PROMOTED_LOCAL-grade evidence
under the two-tier closure rule, not a clean confirm-set result.  The
LFM_V11_EXACTDP package (already packaged, id 56694) carries this head;
its platform score remains the only official-domain measurement.

## 2. Native-framerate M01 control (running)

Premise measured first: all 2,262 PHD2 fragment sources have their media
on disk (5,886 fragments, 100 percent coverage) - the native-framerate
experiment is not media-blocked.

Design: per fragment, decode the source over [t0, t0+L], split into 4
equal sub-windows, 16 native frames each (~8 fps at 30 fps sources, 4x
the keyframe-grid time resolution), VideoMAE-encode each sub-window under
TWO conditions: ordered frames versus the sub-window middle frame
repeated (same decode, zero motion).  Labels: sub-window center GT,
replicated over slots.  Paired arms, same TCN on 32-step inputs.

Status: DONE.  Extraction covered 5,884 of 5,886 mixed fragments
(4 cards, ~33 min).  Control result (1,292 train / 1,329 held-out,
paired seed/stream, MSE objective, 32-step TCN):

| Arm | AP | keep-0.80 sim F1 |
|---|---:|---:|
| ord (native 4x16-frame windows) | 0.6406 | 0.5972 |
| rep (middle frame repeated)     | 0.5611 | 0.5965 |

Findings:
1. The AP gap ord-minus-rep is +0.0795 at native resolution versus
   +0.003 at the 1 fps grid.  Motion evidence EXISTS in the VideoMAE
   representation and is readable ONLY at native time resolution - the
   1 fps keyframe grid destroys it before the encoder sees it.
2. The AP advantage does NOT yet convert to the truncated decision:
   keep-0.80 sim F1 ties (0.5972 vs 0.5965).  Rank quality moved;
   truncation utility did not.
3. The two experiments (1 fps versus native) are NOT comparable in
   absolute level - different label protocols (8 keyframe labels versus
   4 sub-window-center labels) and different fragment pools.  Only the
   WITHIN-experiment paired gaps are comparable.

Next step this motivates: train the exact expected-F objective on the
native ord features - it is exactly the tool that converts ranking
quality into truncation utility, and it just showed a stable +0.035
when given the same chance on the pooled features.

One seed, PAUSED_EXPLORATORY scope; paired control included.


## 3. The intersection experiment: native ord x exactDP (DONE, mixed)

Arms: native 32-step features, {ord, rep} x {MSE, exact-DP}, paired
seeds 20261009/10/11, plus an lr sensitivity check for the DP loss.

| Arm | lr | AP (3 seeds) | keep-0.80 F1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ord_mse | 1e-3 | 0.578 +- 0.049 (0.6406/0.5503/0.5427) | 0.577 mean |
| ord_dp  | 1e-3 | 0.526 +- 0.002 - SATURATED | 0.547 mean |
| rep_dp  | 1e-3 | 0.526 (identical to ord_dp - saturation) | 0.547 |
| ord_dp  | 1e-4 | 0.6432 +- 0.004 | 0.5975 all seeds |

Findings:
1. lr 1e-3 is too large for the DP loss on 32-step sequences: it
   saturates to a bad attractor that IGNORES the features (ord and rep
   converge to the identical solution).  At lr 1e-4 the DP arm recovers
   and becomes the most seed-stable arm in this whole study
   (AP sd 0.004 versus MSE 0.049).
2. Paired ord_dp(lr 1e-4) minus ord_mse(lr 1e-3): +0.007/+0.094/+0.095,
   3/3 positive - but the MSE baseline was run at only one lr, so this
   comparison is confounded by the lr choice.  Recorded as indicative.
3. The blocking observation: EVERY arm's keep-0.80 F1 locks at
   0.595-0.598 regardless of AP (0.52 to 0.65).  The truncation decision
   at this granularity is bounded by the LABELS: 4 sub-window-center
   binaries replicated over 8 slots.  AP measures whole-sequence
   ranking; the top-80 percent cut is dominated by the 4-value label
   grid.  This is the D1 (occupancy) issue in native form: the DP
   objective wants per-block gains, and the current labels give it a
   4-level staircase.

Next step (queued): retrain native ord + DP with occupancy labels
q_t = GT-frame share per sub-window (the D1 direction the V10 review
flagged as exactly the target the expected-F objective consumes), and
finer sub-windows (8x16 native frames).  Also rerun ord_mse at lr 1e-4
to deconfound the objective comparison.

Verdict: native x DP is NOT negative - it is label-bound.  The
pooled-feature DP result (+0.035, 3 seeds, CI [+0.029,+0.041]) stands
unaffected.

## 4. Occupancy-label experiment (DONE) and a correction

All four arms retrained at lr 1e-4 on the SAME native features, paired
seeds, then evaluated on binary labels.  Pool note: the occupancy filter
(float) admits 1,421/1,441 fragments versus 1,292/1,329 for the binary
filter - pools differ, so absolute numbers are NOT comparable to
section 3; only within-experiment comparisons count.

| Arm | AP | keep-0.80 F1 |
|---|---|---|
| dp_bin  | 0.7845 +- 0.0016 | 0.7259 +- 0.0001 |
| dp_occ  | 0.7467 +- 0.0568 | 0.7115 +- 0.0254 |
| mse_bin | 0.7690 +- 0.0162 | 0.7256 +- 0.0002 |
| mse_occ | 0.7725 +- 0.0101 | 0.7234 +- 0.0017 |

Findings and corrections:
1. CORRECTION to section 3: the F1 lock at 0.597 was caused mainly by
   lr 1e-3, not by label granularity alone - mse_bin at lr 1e-4 reads
   0.7256 on its (different) pool.  The lr sensitivity applies to BOTH
   objectives; the "4-level staircase" contribution is secondary.
2. dp_bin (binary labels + DP at lr 1e-4) is the best native
   configuration: highest AP, near-zero seed variance, paired AP deltas
   versus mse_bin +0.032/+0.010/+0.005 (3/3 positive).  F1 ties MSE -
   the DP advantage remains an AP/stability advantage in this regime.
3. Occupancy labels do NOT help in either objective (dp_occ and
   mse_occ are worse with larger seed spread).  The GPT-6-PRO gradient-
   equivalence claim holds for MSE (bin 0.7256 vs occ 0.7234); for DP
   the continuous gains make the 4-block optimization harder.
4.queued: 8 sub-window refinement (8-level staircase), and the
   deployment-side native pipeline (source-video decode for the 426
   drop), which is what any native package would need.

## 5. 8-sub-window refinement (DONE, negative at the current recipe)

Features re-extracted: 5,885 fragments, 8 sub-windows x 16 native
frames, 64-step inputs (6 cards, ~44 min).  Same recipe as the winning
4-window run (dp_bin / mse_bin at lr 1e-4, paired seeds 09/10/11):

| Arm | AP (3 seeds) | F1 |
|---|---|---|
| 8w dp_bin  | 0.436 / 0.467 / 0.516 | ~0.51 |
| 8w mse_bin | 0.497 / 0.508 / 0.496 | ~0.52 |

BOTH objectives collapse to near-random - this is a recipe fit problem,
not a DP problem: the TCN receptive field (15) covers only a quarter of
the 64-step input, and 900 steps may be short for it.  The 4-window
configuration (RF covers half the sequence) stays the native-line
optimum: AP 0.7845 +- 0.0016, paired 3/3 over mse_bin.

Reopen condition for 8 windows: dilated TCN with dils (1,2,4,8,16)
(RF 63) and/or more steps, lr re-scanned for the longer sequence.
