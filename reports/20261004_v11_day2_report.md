## 1. Expected-F three-seed confirmation (DONE)

Design: paired arms (same TCN, same sampler stream), MSE incumbent versus
exact expected-F DP, three seeds, frozen valid-token features, 1,963
train / 1,954 held-out mixed fragments, keep-0.80 simulated F1.

| Arm | seed 20261006 | seed 20261007 | seed 20261008 | mean +- sd |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| MSE keep-0.80 F1 | 0.6273 | 0.6276 | 0.6514 | 0.6354 +- 0.0138 |
| exact-DP keep-0.80 F1 | 0.6625 | 0.6626 | 0.6625 | 0.6625 +- 0.0001 |
| paired delta | +0.0352 | +0.0350 | +0.0111 | +0.0271 |

Per-fragment paired bootstrap (seed 20261006, 10,000 resamples):
delta +0.0352, CI95 [+0.0291, +0.0413].  The CI lower bound is 4x the
preregistered practical delta (0.007).

Readings:
1. The expected-F gain is stable across seeds (3/3 positive).
2. The DP arm's seed variance is near zero (sd 0.0001 vs MSE 0.0138):
   directly maximizing F converges to the same solution regardless of
   initialization, while the ranking surrogate scatters.  A second,
   unplanned, advantage of optimizing the deployment metric.
3. AP moves the same direction (+0.018 to +0.041 paired per seed).

Validity scope: PHD2 in-domain; the 984 held-out sources are
dev-exposed (V11 section 1.5), so this is PROMOTED_LOCAL-grade evidence
under the two-tier closure rule, not a clean confirm-set result.  The
LFM_V11_EXACTDP package (already packaged, id 56694) carries this head;
its platform score remains the only official-domain measurement.

## 2. Native-framerate M01 control (running)

Premise measured first: all 2,262 PHD2 fragment sources have their media
on disk (5,886 fragments, 100 percent coverage) - the native-framerate
experiment is not media-blocked.

Design: per fragment, decode the source over [t0, t0+L], split into 4
equal sub-windows, 16 native frames each (~8 fps at 30 fps sources, 4x
the keyframe-grid time resolution), VideoMAE-encode each sub-window under
TWO conditions: ordered frames versus the sub-window middle frame
repeated (same decode, zero motion).  Labels: sub-window center GT,
replicated over slots.  Paired arms, same TCN on 32-step inputs.

Status: DONE.  Extraction covered 5,884 of 5,886 mixed fragments
(4 cards, ~33 min).  Control result (1,292 train / 1,329 held-out,
paired seed/stream, MSE objective, 32-step TCN):

| Arm | AP | keep-0.80 sim F1 |
|---|---:|---:|
| ord (native 4x16-frame windows) | 0.6406 | 0.5972 |
| rep (middle frame repeated)     | 0.5611 | 0.5965 |

Findings:
1. The AP gap ord-minus-rep is +0.0795 at native resolution versus
   +0.003 at the 1 fps grid.  Motion evidence EXISTS in the VideoMAE
   representation and is readable ONLY at native time resolution - the
   1 fps keyframe grid destroys it before the encoder sees it.
2. The AP advantage does NOT yet convert to the truncated decision:
   keep-0.80 sim F1 ties (0.5972 vs 0.5965).  Rank quality moved;
   truncation utility did not.
3. The two experiments (1 fps versus native) are NOT comparable in
   absolute level - different label protocols (8 keyframe labels versus
   4 sub-window-center labels) and different fragment pools.  Only the
   WITHIN-experiment paired gaps are comparable.

Next step this motivates: train the exact expected-F objective on the
native ord features - it is exactly the tool that converts ranking
quality into truncation utility, and it just showed a stable +0.035
when given the same chance on the pooled features.

One seed, PAUSED_EXPLORATORY scope; paired control included.
