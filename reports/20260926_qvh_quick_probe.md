# QVHighlights local weak-label quick probe (2026-09-26)

This is a bounded data-ingestion and representation control, not an AIC
official evaluation. The audited local asset is the
`video_highlight` directory (QVHighlights-derived weak labels). No official
test video was used.

## Data and protocol

- Source asset: `/home/hajimi2025/datasets/data-challenge-2026/video_highlight`.
- Deterministic subset: first 40 matched `dataset_split=train` records and
  first 10 matched `dataset_split=val` records in annotation-file order.
- Original source clips were copied to `/data/aic/datasets/QVHighlightsQuick`.
- Sampling: 2 FPS; 300 sampled frames per 150-second clip.
- Labels: `qvh_seed_timeline_linear_v1`, generated by linear interpolation of
  the supplied `teacher_signals.timeline.highlight_score`. These are weak seed
  labels and are not AIC joint ground truth.
- Temporal head: the existing `TemporalUNet`, 20 epochs, seed `20260926`,
  batch size 1. Visual backbones remained frozen; there was no fine-tuning.

## Results

All metrics below are validation video-macro F1 and mean per-video Spearman on
the QVHighlights weak timeline proxy. They are not `F_video`.

| Representation | Feature bytes (source file) | Best epoch | F1 | Spearman | Empty rate | Head time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ResNet18 | 44,782,331 | 13 | 0.617799 | 0.395152 | 0.0 | 23.75 s |
| DeiT-S/16 | 86,722,803 | 6 | 0.678463 | 0.393041 | 0.0 | 29.25 s |
| ViT-B/16 | 346,328,529 | 8 | 0.603881 | 0.394206 | 0.0 | 21.63 s |

The frozen DeiT-S control has a +0.060664 proxy F1 margin over ResNet18 on
this single bounded split, while Spearman is effectively unchanged. ViT-B
does not improve this probe. The F1 gap therefore cannot be interpreted as a
stable ranking or official-score gain; it is compatible with calibration and
weak-label effects. The experiment is retained as an ingestion/model-scale
diagnostic only.
